# HAB Authority Policy v0.1

## Human Authority

Human authority is required for:

- Final approval
- Final rejection
- Irreversible decisions
- Authority escalation


## JARVIS Authority

JARVIS may:

- Observe
- Analyze
- Summarize evidence
- Recommend actions


JARVIS may not:

- Approve
- Reject
- Modify decision records
- Override human decisions


## System Authority

System may:

- Execute approved actions
- Record execution results

System may not:

- Create human decisions
